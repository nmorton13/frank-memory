History

A daily cleanup removes old history. Active and pending memories are never removed. Expired rejected items keep a fingerprint so the same text isn't re-imported. Use 0 to keep forever.

Backup

A backup holds the whole vault: every memory with its history and ids, forgotten-text fingerprints, import batches, the guide and its versions, settings, and the activity log. Keep copies somewhere other than Cloudflare. Export MD/JSON above is for reading, not restoring. Connected apps are not in the backup; reconnect them after restoring to a new deployment.
